JCI NEXO

Connect. Lead. Impact.

This document explains what JCI NEXO does and how it works under the hood. For installation, open SETUP.html.

1. The Problem

A JCI chapter loses members quietly — someone stops showing up, their membership lapses, or they age out (JCI membership ends at 40) before anyone reaches out. A chapter president needs to see who is drifting and act before it's too late. JCI NEXO turns scattered attendance and activity data into a single, actionable picture of chapter health.

2. What You See

Dashboard — every member as a row: membership stage, age-out status, attendance, a health score (0–100), and an at-risk flag.

Wellington's Trail — a gamified, points-based progress trail for a selected member, with the AI mascot "Wellington the Wise."

Chapter Pulse — analytics: stage funnel, health distribution, at-risk / mentorship / retention gauges.

Events & Outreach — nearby events, interest-matched members, one-click AI outreach.

Messages — a membership-application inbox with approve / decline + AI-drafted emails.

Alerts — age-out (within 30 days) and at-risk lists.

3. The Health Score

Each member's health is a weighted blend of four signals, normalized to 0–100:

health_score = 0.35·attendance   (depth of event participation)
             + 0.20·recency      (how recently they engaged)
             + 0.15·stage        (progress through membership stages)
             + 0.30·activity      (engagement points, see below)

The four weights must sum to 1. A score at or below the configurable at-risk threshold flags the member. The mascot is reason-aware — it distinguishes a low-engagement alert from an age-out (renewal) alert, and treats an Inactive member as a win-back priority.

4. Engagement Points

Members earn points for real JCI activities. Points feed the activity signal above as clamp(total_points / 300, 0, 1) — 300 points maxes it out.

5. Wellington the Wise (AI)

Wellington powers three AI experiences, all backed by Google Gemini with a deterministic fallback so the app always works — even offline or without a key:

  • Ask Wellington — conversational Q&A grounded strictly in live chapter data (member counts, health, at-risk, stages, badges, engagement points & tiers). It answers only from a factual snapshot, so it never invents members or numbers.
  • Retention advice — for an at-risk member, Wellington writes a diagnosis, recommends real in-window events, and drafts an outreach message. Recommended events are re-verified against the actual search results, so a hallucinated event is discarded before it reaches the president.
  • Outreach & application emails — warm, on-brand invitation, welcome, and decline emails.

6. Architecture

Pure core. backend/member_tracker/core/ holds the domain logic — health score, gamification, activity points, attendance — with no I/O. It's deterministic and easy to reason about.

Single I/O choke point. io/repository.py is the only place that touches SQLite. The Gemini client and synthesizers live in io/ too, isolated behind interfaces.

Thin API. api/ route modules translate HTTP to core/repository calls. The FastAPI app registers each route best-effort.

Frontend. React 19 + Vite, a dependency-free API client (src/api.js), and a dark theme driven by CSS variables. No heavyweight UI framework.

Browser (React)  ──HTTP──▶  FastAPI routes  ──▶  Repository (SQLite)
                                    │
                                    └──▶  Gemini (optional) ──▶ deterministic fallback

7. Safety & Governance

  • Secrets via git-ignored .env; admin endpoints require an X-API-Key.
  • The LLM is optional and time-bounded; every AI path degrades gracefully to a deterministic result.
  • AI event recommendations are verified against real data — no fabricated events.
  • The annual Handover Quest captures an immutable chapter snapshot for a clean leadership transition.
